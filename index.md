# Research Data Governance Kit (RDGkit)

Welcome to the **Research Data Governance Kit**. This toolkit provides guidance, requirements, roles, tools, and best practices for managing data governance across the entire research data life cycle.

## Data Life Cycle Phases

Explore the core phases of the data governance framework below:

* **[0. Global project definition](pages/lifecycle/0_definition.md)** — Initial stage where project objectives, roles and responsibilities, quality and supervision mechanisms, and the scope of the data involved are established.
* **[1. Legal and ethical aspects (ELSI)](pages/lifecycle/1_ethic_legal_aspects_elsi.md)** — Management of ethical committees, consents, regulatory compliance, and secure processing of sensitive data.
* **[2. Collection](pages/lifecycle/2_data_collection.md)** — Definition and standardized collection of essential data, applying quality, traceability, and FAIR principles.
* **[3. Normalization, Standardization, Harmonization](pages/lifecycle/3_normalization_standarization_harmonization.md)** — Transformation processes (ETL), versioning, naming conventions, and practices ensuring reproducibility.
* **[4. Availability](pages/lifecycle/4_availability.md)** — Storage solutions, centralized/hybrid/federated data management, back-ups, and traceability.
* **[5. Discoverability](pages/lifecycle/5_discoverability.md)** — DCAT catalogues, metadata definition, sensitivity classification, and visibility policies.
* **[6. Access](pages/lifecycle/6_access.md)** — Data Access Committees (DAC), access policies, cloud-based analysis vs. download, and robust authentication.
* **[7. Processing and Analysis](pages/lifecycle/7_processing_and_analysis.md)** — Federated and centralized processing, inputs/outputs, secure environments, and the 5-safes framework.
